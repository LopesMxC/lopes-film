# OpenFilm's library

Everything in OpenFilm's library can be used in any film, commercial or not, changed and shared, with no need to
credit anyone.

- The transitions, effects, text, stickers that move, filters and templates are made by the OpenFilm contributors:
  the pages written by hand, the pictures drawn by OpenFilm's renderer (`scripts/library-previews.mjs`) over stills
  made for OpenFilm.
- The sounds and music (`audio/`) are OpenFilm's.

## Sound Pack

The 99 sounds in `sounds/pack-*.mp3` come from the Sound Pack that Lopes supplied (converted from WAV to MP3). Their terms are those of the pack's own author, not the CC0 dedication above.

The UI sounds `sounds/uisfx-*.mp3` are from [UISFX](https://github.com/romainsimon/uisfx) (romainsimon), dedicated to the public domain under CC0 1.0 (`LICENSE-UISFX.txt`). The Kenney packs (CC0) are fetched on request (Free packs).

The sci-fi sounds `sounds/kenney-scifi-*.mp3` and the smoke sprites `vfx/smoke/` are by [Kenney](https://kenney.nl), CC0 1.0.
