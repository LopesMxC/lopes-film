# Shorts (9:16)

A short is judged in the first second and finished in under a minute. Plan it in beats, not in scenes.

## Shape
- **Hook (0–2 s)**: the most surprising, specific or useful line or image of the whole piece, first. No logo, no intro, no "hi, today I'll…". If the strongest moment is at 0:40, it moves to 0:00.
- **Body**: one idea, three to five beats. Each beat changes something on screen (cut, punch-in, caption, b-roll) every 1.5–3 s.
- **Payoff**: say it plainly in the last 3–5 s. End on a held frame or a line that loops back to the first (the last frame can equal the first).
- **Length**: 15–35 s for a single idea, up to 60 s for a story or a tutorial. Cut until it hurts, then keep it.

## Frame (1080 × 1920)
- Safe area: keep faces and captions out of the top 12% and the bottom 22% (the app's own buttons and text sit there) and 5% at the sides.
- Eyes at about the upper third. A horizontal source is cropped to the speaker, not letterboxed; track the face if it moves.
- Captions in the middle-lower area (around 60–70% of the height), 1–3 words per beat, one emphasised word in colour. Always on: most people watch muted.
- Type at least 6% of the frame height. Contrast over footage from a scrim or a thin outline, not a different font.

## Cut a long video into shorts
1. Read the transcript; list every moment that stands alone with a claim, a story or a laugh.
2. Keep the ones that make sense with no context. Each starts at the claim, not the lead-up.
3. Trim every breath and filler; cover the jumps with a punch-in (100% → 115%) or a b-roll of 1.5–3 s.
4. Title card only if it adds the missing context in six words or fewer.

## Sound
Speech clean and loud, music 15–20 dB under it, ducked on speech. One effect on each reveal; none on every cut.

## Check
`lopesfilm look` at 0 s (is it a hook?), at each caption change (is the safe area clear?), and at the last frame. Watch it muted. If you would swipe away at any second, change that second.
