# Media

`lopesfilm get`: media for the film. First Lopes.Film's own library, free, offline and CC0: `get sfx-search`, `get transition`, `get effect`, `get filter`, `get sticker`, `get text` and `get template`, each `--prompt "<what>"` in any words and language; each match says what of your prompt it has and lacks, and `--take <id>` copies the one you choose into the project and prints what to put in film.html. Look there before writing a transition, an effect, a text or a template yourself. The library has no music of its own: music is the person's own (their files in the project's media: look in `assets/audio/` and ask which to use), a song they name (`get youtube-music`), or one made to the prompt. The rest is made by a service the person connected in Studio (Settings → Services): voice-over, music and sound effects to your prompt and length (`get music`, `get sfx`, at a cost), transcripts, images, image search, video and web search. For a real song or a track by an artist, `get web-music --prompt "<mood or title>" --out <name>` searches Jamendo (the person's key), downloads licensed music into `assets/audio/music/` and prints each track's credit: put it in film.html as an `<audio>` clip (a media fragment `#t=in,out` plays the part you need) and credit it in the film's end. Noisy or dull speech: `get audio-clean --src <recording> --out <name> [--denoise light|medium|strong] [--voice] [--eq voice|podcast|warm|bright|bass-cut|telephone] [--hum 50|60] [--normalize -16|off]` runs on this computer (free, no service), writes `assets/audio/clean/<name>.m4a`; use that file in film.html in place of the original. Soft or small picture or clip: `get upscale --src <file> --out <name> [--scale 2|3|4] [--style photo|anime|fast]` makes it larger and sharper on this computer (a clip of about 30 s at most, minutes of work), writes `assets/image/<name>.png` or `assets/video/<name>.mp4`. A person on screen looking away from the lens (down at a script, to the side): `get eyes --src <clip> --out <name> [--strength 0-1]` moves the eyes back to the lens on this computer, the sound kept, and writes `assets/video/<name>.mp4`; use the new file in film.html in place of the original and say so. A video or its sound from a link (YouTube, Vimeo, Instagram, TikTok, X and about a thousand sites): `get web-download --url <link> [--out <name>] [--audio] [--height 720] [--from <s> --to <s>]` fetches it on this computer with yt-dlp into `assets/video/` (or `assets/audio/` as MP3); only for what the person has the right to use (their own upload, a licence that allows it, or a reference to study, the breakdown skill). A song by its name or YouTube link: `get youtube-music --query "artist - title" [--out <name>] [--from <s> --to <s>]` saves it as MP3 in `assets/audio/music/` (the first YouTube result; credit it, and only what the person has the right to use). Voice and music apart: `get stems --src <file> --out <name> --mode voice|instrumental` (demucs when installed). Hearing a sound before choosing it: the listen skill. When the person linked their browser, `get browse --url <https://…>` opens a reference in it so they see it too. Make music or a sound when the person asks for something original, when it must fit the picture exactly, or when the matches lack what was asked; when the person says which they want, do that. `get` alone lists what works now; for a kind not connected, tell the person which services make it and where they connect one. A video clip costs a lot: the person confirms it in Studio first. Its `--help` lists the verbs and their options.

Media can come from `get` (Lopes.Film's library, or a service that makes it), any service the person uses (with its API docs and their key), or your own code. Save it under `assets/`.

When the person wants a service that is not built in to make pictures, clips or sounds (on the canvas too), add it as a provider: `lopesfilm get` ends by naming its folder, the contract it follows and a built-in one to read. Write it from the service's API docs, then try it with `get <verb> --via <id>` once the person has added its key in Settings → Services.
