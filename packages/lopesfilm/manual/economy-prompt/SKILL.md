# Economy: wording

A vague call costs a second round trip.

- Name the file, id, range and output in the call (`src`, `--out`, seconds). Searches are three precise words.
- Ask the person at most once: two or three concrete choices, your recommendation first; carry on with it if they are away. Never ask what the project already answers.
- Decide small things yourself (names, folders, colours, durations); say it in one line.
- Helpers get the goal, the exact files, the answer's format and a size limit, not the history. Plans are one line per step.

Never economize on verifying the final result.
