# Ads and promo videos

An ad makes one person want one thing. Decide four lines before any shot: **who** it is for, **the one promise**, **the proof**, **the action** wanted.

## Structure
| Length | Beats |
| --- | --- |
| 6 s | promise + product + action |
| 15 s | hook (0–2) → problem or desire (2–5) → product doing the thing (5–11) → action (11–15) |
| 30 s | hook → problem → solution shown in use → proof → action |
| 60 s | the 30 s plus a story or a second proof, with a pause before the action |

- The hook is a situation the viewer recognises or a result they want, said in under 8 words. Not the brand name.
- Show the product **working**, in hand, in context, in close-up; not a list of features. One benefit per shot.
- Proof is specific: a number, a before and after, a name, a real quote. "Better" is not proof.
- The action is one thing, said once and shown (button, address, code), held for at least 2 s. Brand mark last, not first.

## Script and text
- Write the script as one-line beats with the time of each, then the on-screen words, then the voice. Voice-over under 2.5 words per second.
- On-screen text repeats the key word only, 2–5 words, in the same place and style every time. Prices and legal lines stay readable for 3 s.
- Numbers and claims must be true and supported by what the person gave you. Never invent a statistic, testimonial, award or discount. Ask when a claim is missing.

## Look
- Take colour, type and tone from the brand (the project's files, the person's logo). One accent colour for the action.
- Product shots: clean background, one light direction, slow push-in (3–6% over the shot). Cut on the movement of the hand or the product.
- Music with a beat that fits the brand; cut on it. Sound effects on every product action (click, pour, snap).

## Formats
Plan for 9:16, 1:1 and 16:9 from the start: keep the product and the words inside the centre 1:1 area, so the same cut can be reframed.

## Check
`lopesfilm look` at the hook, the first product frame, the proof, and the action: can you say the promise after only those four? If not, the ad is still unclear.
